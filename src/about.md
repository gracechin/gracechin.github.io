# About me

I'm Grace Chin and I'm a front-end web developer in the UK. In my spare time, I like to design and making things for the Internet.

## How did I get here?

I first realised I enjoyed coding during university. As part of the Design Engineering program, I got to practice various skills in group design projects. Through these experiences, I found myself especially engaged and involved in the coding and debugging aspects of projects.

My interest in coding grew as I took on several tech roles in small companies. From my colleagues, I learnt how a small tech team can work together to achieve great things.

Curious on how technology and design can help people, I spent over a year as a human-computer interaction design researcher at the School of Design in Hong Kong Polytechnic University. While I learnt a lot from being a researcher and achieved a lot, I missed bringing creative concepts to life through my design and coding skills, as well as the UK. By the end of 2023, I relocated back to the UK and found the job I am currently doing.

## Outside of coding

I live with my husband, Dan. In our spare time, we like to walk and have walked the the big four trails of Hong Kong.
